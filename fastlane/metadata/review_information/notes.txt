3. What the app is

Kilncast is a personal reminder wall for people who keep meaning to write someone and then don’t. Each person is a brick. The wall ranks them by how cold they are likely to be in about seven days, not by last-contact date. You restoke a brick by logging a Touch. Anyone never reached sits on the Ash rail at zero.

It’s for anyone trying to keep up with a small set of people they care about. Everything stays on the device. No account.

4. How to use it

No login, no demo account, no sample files.

1. Launch Kilncast.
2. If onboarding appears, skip it or tap through and Open the kiln.
3. Tap + , enter a name and cadence, then Add to the kiln.
4. Tap a brick (or Restoke on the hero card), pick a channel, write a short note, tap Restoke. An empty note does not heat the brick.
5. Gear icon for settings. Reset all data is there if you want a clean slate.

5. External services

None for the kiln itself. People, Touches, cadence, horizon, and the reminder week are stored on device. Heat is calculated locally.

Settings has Contact Kilncast (https://kilncast.pro/contact-us). Privacy policy: https://kilncast-wall.pro/privacy-policy.

No sign-in, payments, ads, analytics, or AI services.
